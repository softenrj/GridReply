# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. The GridReply project is dedicated to fostering an open and inclusive environment where all contributors and users feel respected and valued.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful** - Treat all members with kindness and respect, regardless of background, identity, or experience level
- **Be Inclusive** - Welcome and support people of all backgrounds and identities
- **Be Constructive** - Provide thoughtful, helpful feedback and engage in good-faith discussions
- **Be Patient** - Remember that this is an educational project; people are here to learn
- **Be Collaborative** - Work together to solve problems and improve the project
- **Give Credit** - Acknowledge the work and contributions of others
- **Be Professional** - Maintain a professional tone in all interactions

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- **Harassment** - Any form of harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- **Discrimination** - Discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or any other protected characteristic
- **Hate Speech** - Use of slurs, offensive language, or derogatory comments
- **Personal Attacks** - Attacking someone's character, appearance, or personal life
- **Trolling** - Intentionally derailing conversations or provoking conflict
- **Spam** - Repetitive or unsolicited content
- **Sexual Misconduct** - Unwelcome sexual advances, jokes, or comments
- **Doxxing** - Sharing private information without consent
- **Threats** - Any form of threats or intimidation
- **Plagiarism** - Presenting others' work as your own

---

## Reporting Guidelines

### If You Witness or Experience Misconduct

1. **Do Not Engage** - Avoid escalating the situation
2. **Document** - Take screenshots or notes if safe to do so
3. **Report Immediately** - Contact the maintainers right away
4. **Provide Details** - Include specific information about what happened

### How to Report

**Email:** rjsharmase@gmail.com  
**Subject:** Code of Conduct Violation Report  
**Include:**
- Description of the incident
- People involved
- When and where it occurred
- Any supporting evidence (messages, screenshots, etc.)
- How you'd like the situation to be handled

---

## Enforcement

### Investigation Process

1. All reports will be taken seriously and investigated promptly
2. The person(s) involved will be given an opportunity to respond
3. All parties will be treated fairly and confidentially
4. A resolution will be communicated to all involved parties

### Possible Outcomes

Depending on the severity and nature of the violation:

- **Verbal Warning** - For minor first-time offenses
- **Written Warning** - For repeated minor violations
- **Temporary Ban** - 30 days to 6 months from the community
- **Permanent Ban** - For severe or repeated serious violations
- **Removal of Content** - Inappropriate comments or posts will be deleted
- **Legal Action** - For serious criminal behavior or threats

## Special Notes for This Educational Project

### For Students
- This is a learning space for all skill levels
- Asking questions is encouraged; there are no "stupid" questions
- Mistakes are part of learning; be kind to those learning
- Help others when you can; remember you were once a beginner

### For Mentors & Experienced Contributors
- Patience with newer contributors is essential
- Provide constructive feedback that helps people grow
- Remember that teaching is a valuable contribution
- Set a positive example for the community

### For Commercial Use Inquiries
If you're interested in using GridReply commercially:
- Email: rjsharmase@gmail.com
- Be respectful of the author's rights
- Understand the educational license terms
- Work with the author professionally to reach an agreement
